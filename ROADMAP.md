# Vision

Project Grundy is a long-term engineering platform designed to provide a production-inspired environment for infrastructure engineering, software development, automation, observability, artificial intelligence, and self-hosted services.

The roadmap outlines the strategic milestones that will transform Project Grundy into a complete, enterprise-grade engineering platform.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every milestone should improve one or more of the following:

- Reliability
- Security
- Simplicity
- Automation
- Documentation
- Observability
- Scalability
- Reproducibility
- Maintainability

# Long-Term Goals

## Reliability

Maintain highly available and recoverable services through redundancy, backups, and monitoring.

---

## Security

Implement layered security through network isolation, secrets management, least-privilege access, and secure defaults.

---

## Observability

Provide complete visibility into infrastructure health, performance, logs, and alerts.

---

## Automation

Reduce manual operational tasks through Infrastructure as Code, automated deployments, and repeatable workflows.

---

## Documentation

Maintain comprehensive documentation for architecture, operations, troubleshooting, and engineering decisions.

---

## Continuous Learning

Use Project Grundy as a platform to evaluate emerging technologies, refine engineering practices, and expand technical expertise.

---

# Success Criteria

Project Grundy will be considered a mature engineering platform when it provides:

- Fully documented infrastructure
- Automated deployments
- Centralized monitoring
- Secure networking
- Reliable backups and disaster recovery
- AI-assisted engineering capabilities
- Reproducible platform builds
- Production-quality operational practices

---

# Looking Ahead

Project Grundy is intended to evolve continuously. Each release will build upon a stable foundation, incorporating new technologies and engineering practices while maintaining a focus on reliability, security, simplicity, and maintainability.

The platform is not intended to be "finished" but to serve as a living engineering environment that grows alongside its operator.y
